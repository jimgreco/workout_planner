Rep, Mix, Burn Privacy Policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

Data Rep, Mix, Burn Stores

Rep, Mix, Burn stores the workout data you create, including exercises, workout routines, training programs, workout logs, gym and equipment lists, settings, and in-app feedback. Sign-in stores the account profile returned by Google or Apple, such as your name, email address, provider ID, and avatar URL when provided.

How The Data Is Used

Your data is used to sync your account across Rep, Mix, Burn web and iOS, operate support workflows, diagnose errors, and maintain and improve the app. Rep, Mix, Burn does not sell your workout data and does not use advertising trackers.

Diagnostics and Service Providers

Feedback can include the app build version. Service logs record technical information about requests and errors, including request identifiers, to help operate and troubleshoot the service. Account data is linked to your sign-in identity. Apple or Google handles sign-in, and our hosting providers process data to run and secure the service. Google Sign-In also declares collection of account contact information (including phone number), approximate location, user and device identifiers, usage information, and other data for authentication, app functionality, and analytics. These provider practices are covered by Google’s Privacy Policy. Rep, Mix, Burn itself does not request location access or a phone number.

Data Controls

You can export your account data or delete your account from the account menu in the app. Deleting the account removes workouts, exercises, routines, programs, gyms, equipment, settings, and feedback stored by Rep, Mix, Burn.

Support

Use the in-app feedback option or TestFlight feedback to report issues. Error messages may include a Request ID, which helps locate the corresponding server logs without sharing private workout details.
