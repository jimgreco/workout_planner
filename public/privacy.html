Forge Privacy Policy

Last updated May 24, 2026

Data Forge Stores

Forge stores the workout data you create, including exercises, workout templates, workout logs, settings, and in-app feedback. Sign-in stores the account profile returned by Google or Apple, such as your name, email address, provider ID, and avatar URL when provided.

How The Data Is Used

Your data is used to sync your account across Forge web and iOS, operate support workflows, diagnose errors, and improve the app during beta testing. Forge does not sell your workout data and does not use advertising trackers.

Data Controls

You can export your account data or delete your account from the account menu in the app. Deleting the account removes workouts, exercises, templates, settings, and feedback stored by Forge.

Support

Use the in-app feedback option or TestFlight feedback to report issues. Error messages may include a Request ID, which helps locate the corresponding server logs without sharing private workout details.
